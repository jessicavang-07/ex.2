Cockatiels

The cockatiel (Nymphicus hollandicus), also known as the weero/weiro or quarrion, is a species of small- tomedium-sized parrot belonging to the cockatoo family (Cacatuidae), of which it is the smallest member. Native to Australia, it is the sole member of the genus Nymphicus.

3 Types of Mutations in Cockatiels

from wikipedia

Cinnamon Cockatiel

The cinnamon mutation, first seen in the 1950s, is very similar in appearance to the grey; however, these birds have a warmer, browner colouring. Pearling was first seen in 1967. This is seen as a feather of one colour with a different coloured edge, such as grey feathers with yellow tips. This distinctive pattern is on a bird's wings or back.

Lutino Cockatiel

Lutino colouration was first seen in 1958. These birds lack the grey of their wild counterparts and are white to soft yellow. This is a popular colour; due to inbreeding, these cockatiels often have a small bald patch behind their crests.

Albino Cockatiel

The albino colour mutation is a lack of pigment. These birds are white with red eyes.
